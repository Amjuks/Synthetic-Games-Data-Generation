# Classic Wordle-compatible vocabulary

The bundled vocabulary is the classic fixed Wordle source-code lexicon:

- `wordle_allowed.txt`: 12,972 accepted guesses; normalized SHA-256 `95b1813ade7abaea175ac9395e88b1e174af2c164b1dd90b716e2379d73bf8bf`.
- `wordle_answers.txt`: 2,315 possible answers; normalized SHA-256 `3a185cbda0d81285d2304eec05aec963af42edb3f4afeb6296c32a41e73a5dd3`.

The word sets were retrieved on 2026-08-14 from the classic lists mirrored by
[deedy/wordle-solver](https://github.com/deedy/wordle-solver) and verified set-equal to
the official lists in [Kinkelin/WordleCompetition](https://github.com/Kinkelin/WordleCompetition).
